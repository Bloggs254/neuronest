Do I need to know exactly what I want?+

No. Tell us what is taking too much time, what your customers need or what you want to achieve. We can work out the right next step together.

Do you work with small businesses?+

Yes. We work with businesses in Kenya and East Africa, including small businesses and ecommerce teams. Larger and international projects are welcome to discuss.

How do you price a project?+

We understand your requirements first, then agree a scope and tailored quote. The form’s budget ranges are conversation starters, not service prices.

Can you connect AI to WhatsApp or email?+

We can scope an assistant around your approved information, with human handover and the agreed messaging integrations. This website’s guide is a local service finder, not live AI chat.

A clear starting point.

This website’s project form and guided service finder prepare an inquiry in your browser. This version does not store your inquiry on a NeuroNest server or send messages automatically.

When you choose WhatsApp or email, your inquiry is passed to that service so you can review and send it. Your use of those services is governed by their own privacy terms. NeuroNest receives the information you choose to send and uses it to discuss your project.

Don’t include passwords, payment details, or sensitive customer records. For questions about your inquiry, contact neuronest@gmail.com.

Optional website feedback also stays in the page until you choose to send an email. Stockroom’s saved workspace is different: it stores inventory records in this browser when you choose My saved records. Read the full privacy information.

Live AI, automated follow-ups, and owner alerts are not enabled in this version.
