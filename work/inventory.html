A clearer view
of your stock.

An inventory system in progress. The aim is simple: make it easier to see what is available, record what changes and spot what needs attention.

The problem
we’re working on.

When product records and stock updates live in different places, even a simple question can take time: what do we actually have available?

This project explores bringing those everyday tasks into one workspace, so a business can check its products, record stock arriving or leaving, and see which items need a closer look.

What works
in the prototype.

The browser prototype lets you search sample products, filter low stock, add a product and record stock coming in or going out. A movement history shows the changes made during the session.

It checks quantities before accepting a movement and prevents stock from going below zero. The focus is on making the routine work clear and keeping the numbers consistent.

Still a project
in progress.

This is a working browser prototype, not a finished inventory service. It uses sample data, and changes reset when the page reloads.

A live version would need saved records, user permissions and testing with the business that will use it. Reporting and integrations would follow the agreed requirements.
