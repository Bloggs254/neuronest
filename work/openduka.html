OpenDuka.
Built for the
trading day.

A phone-first business app for traders, bringing sales, stock, customer credit and suppliers into one workspace.

The work behind
every sale.

A sale is only part of the picture. Stock changes, a customer may still owe money, a supplier needs paying, and the owner needs to understand how the day went.

OpenDuka brings those records together so the next question does not mean searching through separate notebooks, messages and spreadsheets.

A connected
trading workspace.

The working demo includes sales, stock movements, customer credit, suppliers and expenses. Staff roles and approval steps separate routine work from decisions that need an owner.

It also includes reports and an offline workflow that queues work for syncing when the connection returns. Country, currency and language settings support different trading environments.

Working software,
with a clear next step.

OpenDuka is an active project. The screenshots show a fictional business with sample records, so you can see how the software works.

Before a business starts using it, we need to agree hosting, backups, access and security checks. WhatsApp reminders and summaries currently open a draft for someone to review and send.
