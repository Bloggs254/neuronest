Website packages

Entry is KES 35,000, Mid-tier is KES 65,000 and Premium is KES 95,000. The exact inclusions shown on the website service page form the starting point for discussion, not a final quote.

What is not included

Package exclusions remain exactly as listed on the website service page. Work outside an agreed scope is quoted before it begins. No payment, ecommerce, eTIMS, integration, campaign or support commitment should be assumed unless it appears in the written proposal.

Optional care plans

Care plans are optional. Their operations, edit allowance, support channels, response targets and reporting are limited to what is listed on the website service page and confirmed in writing. Premium care remains unavailable until its ticketing and monitoring target is proven.
