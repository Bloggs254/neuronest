# NeuroNest: Premium technology studio

Approved direction: premium technology studio, polished and distinctive. This full redesign replaces the blue-and-white split hero and the single-page service summary.

Audience: businesses in Kenya and East Africa seeking websites, AI assistants, workflow automation, operational software or marketing support. Primary action: begin a useful project conversation. No invented client results, logos, timelines or prices.

Identity: deep petrol #082f33, glacier mint #d8eee3, cool white #fbfcfa, silver surface #edf2ef, readable muted teal #526669. Space Grotesk for display, Manrope for body. Both fonts are self-hosted with SIL licenses. Body reading text is generally 16–18px; small interface labels are secondary.

Signature: a commissioned CGI nest sculpture of interlocking brushed aluminum, glacier glass and petrol metal ribbons. This is abstract brand artwork, never represented as client work. Main WebP is approximately 122KB and the mobile version 47KB. The image has a transparent background. On fine-pointer desktop screens the wrapper arrives once over 1.55 seconds with a 24px lift, a restrained 2-degree turn and a 4px soft settle while the image responds immediately to the cursor, using reused 200ms tweens and a maximum 3-degree tilt. Phones and touch devices receive a smaller one-time 1.2-second arrival when the artwork is visible, without cursor tracking. Reduced-motion devices stay still.

Structure: immersive full-width hero, studio introduction, interactive capability explorer, an in-development inventory project, studio process, enquiry form and FAQs. Five detailed service pages, an inventory-project page and privacy page supply substance beyond the homepage. Each service explains uses, possible deliverables, scope and open questions.

Preserved behavior: enquiry review before sending, WhatsApp/email drafts, guide flow and privacy dialog, interrupted-panel safety, mobile navigation, reduced motion and static content fallbacks. The capability explorer is local. Service links preselect only an allowlisted public service slug. No customer information is stored in URLs.

Motion: one short optional hero entrance. The sculpture has no continuous spin or float loop. Its optional entrance and pointer motion are isolated in art-motion.js, reset when inactive and removed when media conditions change. No scroll gating, repeated reveals or mandatory animation. Existing app state remains separate from motion.

Buildless source of truth: dist/. scripts/sync-public.mjs mirrors only known public files and directories. Never copy the repository root or audit folders into deployment. No remote skill engine, automatic hook, paid image API or AI messaging integration is needed to serve this website.

Showcase restraint: the homepage presents two concise editorial previews, with short captions and no live demo controls. The full experimental builds remain on separate optional pages, reached through one quiet link. Service pages contain no demo callout sections. Use “Our work” in navigation. Prioritise a calm business presentation over the breadth of the demo library. Studio-concept and in-progress labels remain factual.

Desktop readability: at widths above 760px use 18px for explanatory reading copy, 16px for navigation, form labels and supporting information, and a slightly stronger body weight. Keep compact illustration labels independent.

Optional demo identities: the NeuroNest header and footer retain the studio branding. Inside each product canvas, Everyday Goods uses cream, terracotta and editorial serif lettering; Frontdesk uses blue conversational bubbles and a separate handover rail; Flowboard uses violet, an asymmetric workbench and a connected step layout; Stockroom uses navy, tabular numbers and a striped ledger; Campaign Kit uses warm gold and a large editorial campaign preview. These are fictional concepts or prototypes, never completed client work. No additional homepage demo blocks.

Trust and project storytelling: replace existing studio copy with a plain introduction and concrete collaboration process. The inventory story uses the real Stockroom prototype screenshot and three short editorial sections, clearly distinguishing working browser behavior from requirements for a live system. Avoid invented founder credentials, clients or outcomes. Keep customer assistant integration deferred.

Problem-led service copy: familiar business questions lead the existing service explorer, with descriptive service names beneath. Keep the hero and service introductions concrete: customer reach, routine questions, repetitive entry, scattered records and useful enquiries. Connect each problem to a possible deliverable without promising sales figures or presenting market research as NeuroNest results. Preserve the five service mappings, static fallback, enquiry review and deferred live assistant.
