# Website feedback and measurement

Status: the website has a voluntary feedback draft behind one footer disclosure. No participants have been contacted, no feedback has been collected, and visitor analytics is not connected. The CSV templates are empty on purpose.

## First review round

Ask five business owners to try the published site on their normal device. Include at least two people outside the technology industry and a mix of service and stock-based businesses. Request consent for taking brief notes; use anonymous participant codes rather than personal or customer details. Do not record sessions without separately agreed consent.

Allow about ten minutes each. Do not explain NeuroNest before the first question or tell participants which answer you want.

1. After a brief look at the homepage: “What do you think this company does?”
2. “Is there anything here that could help your business? Show me where you would look.”
3. “Imagine you wanted to discuss that problem. What would you do next?” Stop before actually sending an enquiry unless they independently wish to contact NeuroNest.
4. “Was anything unclear, hard to read or difficult to use?”
5. “Would you consider contacting the company? What makes you say that?”

For Stockroom, use sample details: find a low-stock product, record an incoming quantity, add a product, export a backup, then explain where records are saved. Do not ask someone to enter confidential business data during a review.

## Record what happened

Use `feedback-round.csv`. Separate successful task completion from the participant's opinion. Record where the person hesitated, not an invented explanation. A stated willingness to contact the company is not a sale. With five participants, findings are qualitative signals, not representative percentages for Kenya.

Prioritise repeated confusion, inability to find a relevant service, difficulty finding contact, misleading project labels and storage/backup misunderstanding. Change one or two important things, then repeat the tasks with new participants. Preserve the two-preview homepage and avoid adding a block for every suggestion.

## Weekly owner review

Use `weekly-review.csv`. Until analytics is connected, leave visits and source fields blank; do not put zero where data is unavailable. Count enquiries actually received in WhatsApp/email, noting only an anonymous reference, service category and whether they were relevant. Keep personal enquiry content in the existing communication channel.

After aggregate analytics is connected, compare the same weekly periods: marketing-page views, service-page interest, useful enquiries received and common feedback themes. Treat contact-link clicks as intent, not completed enquiries. Do not promise a conversion improvement without an adequate baseline and comparable data.

Decide one next action each week: clarify a service, fix an obstacle, improve a project explanation, or leave the page alone if the evidence is weak. Never publish participant statements as testimonials without explicit permission and an accurate attribution.
