Which tools can you connect?

That depends on the tools, their available interfaces and your account permissions. We verify those before committing to an integration.

Does automation remove every manual step?

No. Review and approval often belong with a person, especially when money, customer data or unusual cases are involved.

What happens when a connection fails?

The scope should define retries, alerts and a safe manual path before the workflow is used.
