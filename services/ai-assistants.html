The same questions should not take up your whole day.

Questions about services, opening hours or how to order can interrupt other work. We can scope an assistant for website chat, WhatsApp or email, with clear limits on what it can answer.

A clear scope.
Before a commitment.

We start by understanding your business, the tools you use and the outcome you need. Then we agree on deliverables, responsibilities, integrations, costs and checkpoints.

Hosting, provider subscriptions, messaging accounts and ongoing support are discussed where they apply. No hidden assumption that everything is included.

A few useful
answers.

Can it make decisions or promises for me?+

We define boundaries together. Pricing commitments, sensitive decisions and unsupported answers should go to an authorised person.

Is WhatsApp or email included automatically?+

Each channel needs its own agreed integration, account setup and credentials. Provider rules and ongoing costs are discussed in the scope.

Is this website’s guide live AI?+

No. Our current website guide is a local service finder. A live AI assistant is a separate integration project.
