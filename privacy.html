What this website handles, and what it does not.

This static website does not collect enquiries or run analytics. Email, phone and WhatsApp will remain unavailable until their ownership, monitoring and data-handling procedures are verified.

Static website

The static website serves local fonts, images, styles and scripts. It has no analytics or advertising tracker. Contact channels remain disabled because ownership, monitoring, receipt and reply have not been verified.

Future enquiries

No enquiry form is active, so this website does not currently receive or store customer messages. If enquiries are enabled later, the form will collect a name, phone or WhatsApp number, optional email, selected service and short message so the responsible NeuroNest operator can review and reply. Stockroom browser records

Stockroom can save inventory records in this browser and device. They are not encrypted or synced. Clearing browser data may remove them; downloaded backups remain the visitor’s responsibility. Avoid shared devices and personal or sensitive records.

Access and erasure

There is no public enquiry record to access or erase while contact channels are disabled. A verified request method and operating procedure will be published before customer information is collected. No retention period is stated until it is approved.

Before real enquiries

NeuroNest will verify the responsible inbox, processors, retention period, access and deletion procedure, and any transfer safeguards before activating customer enquiries. The customer notice will be updated when those details are confirmed.
