# Milestone 4 owner gates

Status: no live activation is permitted. Complete and record these decisions before any VPS, DNS, analytics, inquiry or contact-channel work.

## Identity and routing

- Approve the production domain and prove DNS control.
- Verify the public email, phone and security-reporting channel.
- Set `NOTIFY_EMAIL` privately on the future VPS; never commit it.
- Supply and verify the Google Search Console token.

## Hosting and recovery

- Approve the exact provider plan and checkout total after tax and currency conversion.
- Confirm the monthly total remains within the approved cap.
- Approve an encrypted backup destination outside the VPS.
- Approve retention and deletion periods, then complete a scratch restore drill.
- Approve a live-work window and a separate DNS cutover window.

## Privacy and measurement

- Approve the inquiry notice, retention schedule, access/erasure procedure and processor/transfer assessment.
- Approve an analytics provider and consent approach, or explicitly keep analytics disabled.
- Authorize one synthetic live inquiry delivery test before customer enquiries can open.

## Product boundary

- OpenDuka production work remains separate. It requires its reviewed source, authoritative scope and an explicit production authorization.

The machine-readable state is `config/m4-readiness.json`. A false gate must not be changed merely to make a test pass; it represents a recorded owner decision or verified external fact.
