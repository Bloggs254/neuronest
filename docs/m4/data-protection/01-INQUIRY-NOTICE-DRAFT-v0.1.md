# Inquiry data notice — draft v0.1

Not approved for publication or collection. NeuroNest would collect the name, optional business name, reply contact, selected service, message and optional budget/timing only to review and respond to an inquiry. Visitors should not submit passwords, payment details, health information or other sensitive records. The live recipient, lawful basis, retention period, processors, storage location and transfer safeguards remain undecided. Until those items and a monitored channel are approved, the public site must not collect or claim to send inquiries.
