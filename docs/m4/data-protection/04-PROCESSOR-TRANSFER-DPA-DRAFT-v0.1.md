# Processor and transfer assessment — draft v0.1

Not a signed DPA or legal conclusion. Before using a hosting, email, backup or AI provider, record its legal entity, role, processing purpose, data fields, storage/processing countries, subprocessors, security controls, deletion terms, incident notice, audit evidence and international-transfer mechanism. The owner must approve the provider contract and applicable Kenyan data-protection obligations. Consent alone must not be treated as resolving processor or cross-border safeguards.
